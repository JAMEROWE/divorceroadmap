## The Court Filing Fee (everyone pays this)

California charges a first-appearance filing fee of **$435** (as set by Judicial Council Rule 2.1005), though some counties may add minor administrative surcharges on top of this base rate.

> **Verified against official sources:** California Courts Self-Help Center (selfhelp.courts.ca.gov) and your county superior court's fee schedule. These figures were verified for January 2026.

You pay this fee **twice** in some cases: once when filing the petition, and once when filing a response — but never in a true default or joint summary dissolution situation.

## Can You Avoid the Filing Fee? (Yes — the fee waiver)

If paying would create financial hardship, you can ask the court to waive it using form **FW-001**. If approved, the state covers:

- All filing fees
- Service of process fees
- Certified copy fees

Thousands of Californians qualify every year. We cover exactly who qualifies and how to apply in our guide: **[How to Get a Divorce Fee Waiver in California (Form FW-001)](/posts/how-to-get-a-divorce-fee-waiver-in-california/)**.

## Why Amicable Divorces Cost 10x Less

The single biggest cost driver isn't the court — it's **disagreement**. Every contested issue (property, support, custody) means more attorney hours billed at 350–350–350–600/hour depending on region and experience. Couples who agree on terms and file together can complete the entire process for little more than the filing fee.

## Summary Dissolution: The Cheapest Path (if you qualify)

If you've been married under 5 years, have no children, and your community and separate property is under $45,000 (excluding vehicles), **summary dissolution** is a simplified process with less paperwork and one filing.

See the full list of requirements under Family Code §2400 via the [California Courts Self-Help Center](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/summary-dissolution).

### How much does a divorce cost with a lawyer in California?

A **contested divorce with attorneys on both sides typically costs 20,000–20,000–20,000–35,000+ per couple**, billed at 350–350–350–600 per hour. Each disputed issue adds billable hours — which is why agreement is the single biggest money-saver.

### Can I get a divorce for free in California?

Yes — if paying court fees would create financial hardship, file form **FW-001** and the court can waive all filing, service, and certified-copy fees. See our guide to the [California divorce fee waiver](/posts/how-to-get-a-divorce-fee-waiver-in-california/).

### Do you have to pay the filing fee to respond to a divorce?

Usually yes — the respondent pays the same first-appearance fee when filing a response, unless they also qualify for a fee waiver or the case ends in a true default where no response is required.

### What's the cheapest way to get a divorce in California?

**Summary dissolution** if you qualify (married under 5 years, no children, property under $45,000 excluding vehicles) — otherwise, an amicable DIY divorce using a joint petition, with or without a fee waiver.

### How much does it cost to serve divorce papers in California?

A professional process server typically charges **50–50–50–125**. You can reduce or eliminate this: some county sheriff's offices serve for a reduced fee (often waived with a fee waiver), and having any adult over 18 mail the documents costs nothing but postage.
