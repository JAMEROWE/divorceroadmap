Hi, I'm Jamie Rowe — a researcher, not a lawyer.

This site exists because divorce information online is either written by
lawyers for lawyers, or buried in intimidating government pages. Every guide
here is built by reading official state court sources — statutes, court
websites, published fee schedules — and translating them into plain English,
with the verification date noted so you know how current the information is.

**What this site is:** a map — costs, steps, timelines, and the questions
you'll want to ask a licensed attorney in your state.

**What this site is not:** legal advice. Divorce law varies by state and
county and changes over time. Always confirm current details with your
local court or a licensed attorney before acting.
